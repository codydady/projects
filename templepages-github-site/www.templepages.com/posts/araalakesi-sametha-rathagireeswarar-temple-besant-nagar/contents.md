### Sri Rathnagireeswarar Temple, Besant Nagar

**Overview**

Located in the quiet neighborhood of Besant Nagar (near the bus terminus), Sri Rathnagireeswarar Temple is a prominent spiritual center in Chennai, renowned for its strict adherence to Vedic traditions and community service.

**Main Deities**

* **Moolavar:** Sri Rathnagireeswarar 
* **Ambal:** Sri Aralakesi Amman

**Key Highlights & Architecture**

* **West-Facing Sanctum:** Unlike most Shiva temples that face east, the main sanctum and the 5-tiered Rajagopuram face West.
* **Rajagopuram:** A 40-foot-high, 5-tiered entrance tower designed under the guidance of the Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham.
* **Special Shrines:** Features shrines for Sri Adi Shankara, Rathna Vinayagar, Narthana Vinayagar, Rathna Subramaniar (with Valli & Devasena), Kothanda Ramar, Lord Vishnu, Anjaneyar, Dakshinamurthy, Natarajar, the 63 Nayanmars, and the Navagrahas.
* **Annabhishekam:** Famous for its annual Aipasi Pournami Annabhishekam, where offered food items are distributed to charitable homes and orphanages.
* **Vaidika Traditions:** Equipped with a permanent Homa Kundam for regular Chandi Homams, Maharudra Homams, and daily Pradosham rituals.

**Key Specialities**

This temple may look like a new temple in an affluent neighbourhood in Chennai catering to locals while more older and spiritually significant temples in nearby Thiruvanmiyur and Mylapore  sung in hymns like Thevaram , it is not.

When devotees were trying to install deities by ordering them from various sources, Kanchi Maha Periyavar , Sri Chandrashekarendra Saraswathi Swamigal reportedly told them to look at the nearby Bay of Bengal for a lingam long hidden in the ocean. 

With the help of fisherman, they uncovered a lingam submerged which was told to be over a 1000 years old. The Pillaiyar idol is said to have been personally under the possession of Sri Bhagawan Ramana Maharishi!
